# Episode teaser — added options

These two options are added to the existing AMIX episode teaser feature.

They do not replace or redesign the rest of that feature. This document does not change the current architecture and does not authorize an implementation pass.

## 1. Teaser background music

The teaser can play background music under the selected fragments.

Controls:

- Music on/off
- Source: **Epic Cinematic Motivational**, or a custom file the user selects or uploads
- Music volume
- Automatic dialogue ducking

**Epic Cinematic Motivational** is a style, not a copied recording. The mood is epic, cinematic, and motivational, in the manner of a trailer: rising energy, dramatic percussion, tension, and a payoff. The asset must be original or licensed. Do not copy a CapCut track or any other unlicensed commercial recording.

Automatic dialogue ducking keeps speech intelligible while music is on. Music off leaves the dialogue as it is.

## 2. Voice emphasis / voice effect

An optional effect may be applied to a selected important teaser fragment or sentence.

Choices:

- None
- Studio Echo
- Cinematic Echo

Intensity, when an effect is selected:

- Low
- Medium
- High

The effect applies only to that selected fragment or sentence. It is not applied to the whole teaser unless the user selects each range.

The effect is presentation only. Its purpose is to make an important line feel more dramatic. It must not change:

- the spoken words
- speaker identity
- subtitle timing
